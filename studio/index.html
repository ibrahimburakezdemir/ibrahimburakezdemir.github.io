The studio

A small workshop
with a big anvil.

The Happy Smash Iron is an independent studio. We make small mobile games and handy apps, the kind you can learn in a few seconds and keep coming back to.

The name says how we like to work: pick one good idea, put it on the anvil and keep hammering until it feels right. Then we smile, ship it, and start on the next one.

We keep things fair. Our games play offline, need no account, and treat ads as something you can switch off, not something to trap you with.
