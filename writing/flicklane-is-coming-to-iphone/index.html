Flicklane is coming to iPhone

The iPhone version of Flicklane is in App Store review, and the Android version is on its way to Google Play.

31 October 2026 · 1 min read

Until now, Flicklane was an Android-only game. Not anymore: we’ve sent the first iOS version (1.0.0) to Apple for review.

What you get on iPhone

It’s the same game as on Android. Two cars, one tap each, chains of last-second dodges, and Overdrive past the normal speed cap.

  • There’s a banner at the top and an optional rewarded video. Watch it to continue the same run once.
  • On first launch the game shows Apple’s tracking prompt. If you choose “Ask App Not to Track”, you still get the whole game, rewarded Continue included.
  • Ad removal and packs of lights come to iPhone in a later update.

The first version launches in 147 countries. European Union countries aren’t on that list yet; we’ll get there later.

Privacy

We’ve updated our privacy policy to cover iPhone. The game itself still collects no personal data; ads are served by Google AdMob.

What’s next

Apple’s review usually takes a day or two. When the game lands on the App Store and Google Play, we’ll share the download links here.
