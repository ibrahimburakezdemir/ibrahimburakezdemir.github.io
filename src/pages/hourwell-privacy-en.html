Privacy policy

Last updated: 2 November 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how the Hourwell time tracking app for iPhone and Android (“the App”, “we”, “us”), published by The Happy Smash Iron, handles your information.

Hourwell does not collect any data, does not send it to a server, and does not share it with third parties. The App contains no analytics, no advertising ID, no crash reporting service and no tracking. The records you keep stay on your phone.

Where your data is stored

DataWhere it is storedWho can see it
Activities, time entries and notesA database on your phone (hourwell.sqlite). On iPhone it sits in a storage area shared only with Hourwell’s own widgets, Live Activity and shortcuts.Only you
Preferences (theme, pet, language, goals, and your profile name if you set one)A settings file on your phoneOnly you
Pro membership statusA separate settings file on your phoneOnly you
Error log (the last 40 errors)On your phone. It contains error codes and messages, never your activity names or notes.Only you. It is shared only if you copy it yourself with Settings → About → Copy error log and send it to someone.

The App itself does not connect to the internet. The only network traffic is the purchase check done by the App Store (iPhone) or Google Play (Android). Nothing about your records, your settings or how you use the App is sent.

When data leaves your phone

Data leaves your phone only in these cases. You start the first ones yourself; the backups depend on your phone’s own settings:

  1. CSV export. You send the file to the app you choose.
  2. Backup file. It is saved where you choose (for example the Files app).
  3. Cloud sync (Android). When you choose a folder, a single JSON file is written to it. If that folder belongs to a provider such as Google Drive, Dropbox or OneDrive, the file is stored on that provider’s servers and is subject to that provider’s privacy policy. Hourwell has no servers; we never have access to your data. You can stop syncing with Settings → Cloud sync → Disconnect. Cloud sync is not yet available on iPhone.
  4. Siri and Shortcuts (iPhone). If you use Hourwell with Siri, Siri sees the activity names you say or pick and the answer it reads to you (for example today’s total). Siri requests are handled by Apple under Apple’s privacy policy. Nothing is sent to us.
  5. iPhone backup. If iCloud Backup is on, or you back up your iPhone to a computer, the App’s data is included in that backup, managed by Apple; Hourwell cannot access it. You can turn it off in Settings → [your name] → iCloud → iCloud Backup.
  6. Android backup. If Google backup is turned on for your phone, Android backs up the App’s data (your records and settings) to your Google account and restores it when you move to a new phone. This backup is managed by Google and, on Android 9 and later, is encrypted with your screen lock if you have one set; Hourwell cannot access it. You can turn it off in your phone’s Settings → System → Backup.

Purchases

Subscriptions and one-time purchases are handled by the App Store on iPhone and by Google Play on Android. The App does not see, store or process your payment details; those are between you and Apple or Google. The only thing the App reads from the store is whether you have Pro access.

On Android the App does not ask for the exact alarm permission: the reminder and alerts use normal alarms, which may arrive a few minutes late.

On either platform the App does not ask for access to your location, contacts, photos, camera, microphone, health data or device identifiers.

Children

The App is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect data from children.

Deleting your data

Deleting the App deletes all of its data on your phone, including the data its widgets use. You can delete the hourwell-sync.json file written to your cloud folder from that folder yourself.
