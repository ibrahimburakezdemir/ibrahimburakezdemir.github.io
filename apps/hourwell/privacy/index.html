Apps / Hourwell / Privacy

Privacy policy

Last updated: 15 October 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how the Hourwell time tracking app (“the App”, “we”, “us”), published by The Happy Smash Iron, handles your information.

Hourwell does not collect any data, does not send it to a server, and does not share it with third parties. The App contains no analytics, no advertising ID, no crash reporting service and no tracking. The records you keep stay on your phone.

Where your data is stored

DataWhere it is storedWho can see it
Activities, time entries and notesA database on your phone (hourwell.sqlite)Only you
Preferences (theme, pet, language, goals, and your profile name if you set one)A settings file on your phoneOnly you
Pro membership statusA separate settings file on your phoneOnly you
Error log (the last 40 errors)On your phoneOnly you. It is shared only if you copy it yourself with Settings → About → Copy error log and send it to someone.

The App itself does not connect to the internet. The internet permission in the app package comes from the Google Play Billing library and is used only to verify purchases. Nothing about your records, your settings or how you use the App is sent.

Files you export

Data leaves your phone in four cases. You start the first three yourself; the fourth depends on your phone’s own backup setting:

  1. CSV export. You send the file to the app you choose.
  2. Backup file. It is saved to the folder you choose.
  3. Cloud sync. When you choose a folder, a single JSON file is written to it. If that folder belongs to a provider such as Google Drive, Dropbox or OneDrive, the file is stored on that provider’s servers and is subject to that provider’s privacy policy. Hourwell has no servers; we never have access to your data. You can stop syncing with Settings → Cloud sync → Disconnect.
  4. Android backup. If Google backup is turned on for your phone, Android backs up the App’s data (your records and settings) to your Google account and restores it when you move to a new phone. This backup is managed by Google and, on Android 9 and later, is encrypted with your screen lock if you have one set; Hourwell cannot access it. You can turn it off in your phone’s Settings → System → Backup.

Purchases

Subscriptions and one-time purchases are handled by Google Play. The App does not see, store or process your payment details; those are between you and Google. The only thing the App reads from Google Play is whether you have Pro access.

Permissions

PermissionWhy
NotificationsThe running timer, the end of a Pomodoro, the daily reminder and the forgotten-timer alert
Foreground service (Android 14 and later only)Keeps the timer notification and the Pomodoro chime going while a timer runs, even with the screen off
Run at startupBrings back the running timer notification and the reminder after the phone restarts
VibrationThe Pomodoro end notification
Keep awakeLets the reminder and the forgotten-timer alert arrive even while the phone is asleep
Internet, network state, billingOnly to verify purchases with Google Play
File picker (Storage Access Framework)Access only to the files and folders you choose

The App does not ask for the exact alarm permission: the reminder and alerts use normal alarms, which may arrive a few minutes late.

The App does not ask for access to your location, contacts, camera, microphone or device identifiers.

Children

The App is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect data from children.

Deleting your data

Uninstalling the App deletes all of its data on your phone. You can delete the hourwell-sync.json file written to your cloud folder from that folder yourself.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, this page will be updated and the date at the top will change.
