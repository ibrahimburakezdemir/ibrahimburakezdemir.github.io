Time tracker · Android

What it does

Calm on the outside, precise underneath.

  • Many timers at onceStudy, work, exercise, reading: each keeps its own colour and emoji, and keeps running while the screen is locked.
  • A screen that breathesFive living themes, from a desert oasis to a bubbling lab, and a small pet that naps while your timer runs.
  • Goals and focusSet a daily goal or limit per activity, and a Pomodoro length that chimes when the time is up.
  • Clear statisticsDay, week and month views, bar and pie charts, a streak counter and a heatmap of the last 12 weeks.
  • Your data stays yoursNo ads, no tracking, no account. Records stay on your phone; export to CSV or sync through a cloud folder you choose.
  • Right where you need itA home screen widget, a quick settings tile, a daily reminder, light and dark themes, and 24 languages.
